# Privacy Policy for BTS Delivery

**Last Updated: October 25, 2025**

## Introduction

BTS Delivery ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the App.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide to Us

**Account Information**
- Email address
- Name
- Profile information
- Authentication credentials

**Delivery and School Information**
- School names and addresses
- Delivery schedules and times
- Delivery notes
- Contact information for schools
- Staff assignments

**Photos and Documents**
- Classroom setup photos
- School maps (images or PDFs)
- Proof of delivery documentation
- Digital signatures

**Calendar Data**
- Delivery dates and schedules
- Calendar sync preferences (if enabled)

### Information Collected Automatically

**Device Information**
- Device type and model
- Operating system version
- Unique device identifiers
- Mobile network information

**Usage Data**
- App features used
- Time spent in the app
- Crash reports and performance data
- Error logs

**Location Data**
- We do NOT collect precise location data
- Location permissions may be requested by dependencies but are not actively used

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

1. **Provide and Maintain the App**
   - Create and manage user accounts
   - Process and track deliveries
   - Manage school and classroom information
   - Enable photo uploads and document management

2. **Improve the App**
   - Analyze app usage and performance
   - Fix bugs and crashes
   - Develop new features
   - Optimize user experience

3. **Communication**
   - Send delivery notifications
   - Provide customer support
   - Send important updates about the App

4. **Security and Compliance**
   - Prevent fraud and abuse
   - Comply with legal obligations
   - Protect user safety

## Data Storage and Security

### Firebase Services

We use Google Firebase to store and process your data:
- **Firebase Authentication**: Secure user authentication
- **Cloud Firestore**: Database for app data
- **Firebase Storage**: Photo and document storage
- **Firebase Analytics**: App usage analytics
- **Firebase Crashlytics**: Crash reporting

Your data is stored on secure Google Cloud servers. Firebase complies with industry-standard security practices and regulations.

### Data Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures, including:
- Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
- Encryption of data at rest
- Role-based access control
- Regular security assessments
- Secure authentication practices

However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### We Share Your Information:

**With Your Team**
- Other users within your organization can access shared delivery and school information

**With Service Providers**

**For Legal Reasons**
- To comply with legal obligations
- To protect our rights and safety

### We Do NOT:
- Sell your personal information to third parties
- Share your data for advertising purposes
- Use your data for purposes unrelated to the App's functionality

## Your Rights and Choices

### Account Access and Control
- **Access**: You can access your account information within the App
- **Update**: You can update your profile information at any time
- **Delete**: You can request account deletion by contacting us

### Photo and Document Management
- You can delete photos you've uploaded

## Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

## California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have specific rights regarding your personal information:

- **Right to Know**: What personal information we collect and how it's used
- **Right to Delete**: Request deletion of your personal information
- **Right to Opt-Out**: Opt-out of the sale of personal information (Note: We do not sell personal information)
- **Right to Non-Discrimination**: Equal service regardless of privacy rights exercise

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.

## GDPR Compliance (European Users)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

- **Legal Basis**: We process data based on consent and contractual necessity
- **Data Subject Rights**: Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection
- **Data Retention**: We retain data only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined
- **International Transfers**: Data may be transferred to countries outside the EEA with appropriate safeguards

## Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as:
- Your account is active
- Necessary to provide services
- Required by law or for legitimate business purposes

When you delete your account:
- Personal information is deleted or anonymized
- Some data may be retained for legal compliance or audit purposes
- Deletion is typically completed within 30 days
